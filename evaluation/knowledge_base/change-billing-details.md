# Updating your payment method

To change the credit card or bank account we charge, open Settings > Billing > Payment method and choose "Replace". The new payment method is used from the next invoice onward.

If a payment fails, we retry three times over seven days and email the workspace owner after each attempt. The workspace stays active during the retries.

You can also change the billing address and VAT number on the same page; the changes appear on future invoices only.
