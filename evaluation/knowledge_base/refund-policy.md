# Refund policy

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. A refund usually takes five to ten business days to appear on your bank statement, depending on your bank.

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are refunded pro rata for the unused months, minus any discount that was applied at checkout.

Monthly plans are not refunded. You can cancel a monthly plan at any time and keep access until the end of the current billing period.

To request a refund, open Settings > Billing > Refund request and choose the invoice you want refunded. A billing specialist reviews every request within two business days.
