# Cancelling your subscription

Workspace owners can cancel from Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Cancelling stops automatic renewal: you will not be billed again, and the workspace stays fully usable until the end of the period you already paid for.

After the period ends the workspace becomes read-only for 60 days. During that time you can still export your data or reactivate the subscription with one click, and nothing is lost.

If you only want fewer paid seats, remove members instead of cancelling. Seat changes apply to the next invoice.
