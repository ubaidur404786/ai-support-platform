# Resetting your password

On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password" and enter the email address you registered with. We send a reset link that expires after one hour.

If the reset email does not arrive within ten minutes, check your spam folder and make sure the address is spelled correctly. Company mail filters sometimes hold our messages; ask your IT team to allow mail from our domain.

Passwords must be at least 12 characters. For security, resetting your password signs you out of every other device.
