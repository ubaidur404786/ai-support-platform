# Personal data requests (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct and erase the personal data we hold about you. Send your request to privacy@example.com from the email address on your account.

We answer access and erasure requests within 30 days. Erasure removes your personal data from the product immediately and from backups within 90 days.

If you are a member of a workspace owned by your employer, some requests must go through the workspace owner.
